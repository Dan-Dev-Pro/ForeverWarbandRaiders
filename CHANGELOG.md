# Changelog

## 0.5.0
- Best in slot per gear set: caps first (hit, spell hit, defense, in that order), then your stat weights
- Blessing of Kings and other % bonuses raise primary stats, so they beat flat attack power at similar value
- Compares Dungeon Journal drops and dungeon quest rewards plus everything you own, wear, plan or favourite
- Class weapon and armour rules, weapon style per set, unique items, pins for slots you want to keep
- Weights view with starter templates; settings boxes now save when you click away

## 0.4.0
- Blizzard-style window: portrait, title bar, inset panels, tabs along the bottom, native slot frames
- Two-column layouts on every tab, less scrolling
- Gear: character on the left, Drops / Favourites / Plan panel on the right; drops show item icons, grouped by dungeon, nine per page
- Plan sharing moved to Settings

## 0.3.0
- Gear sets laid out like the character window: slots down both sides, weapons along the bottom, coloured by status
- 3D preview of your character wearing the planned set
- Drag items onto slots; right-click to clear; ctrl-click opens the Dungeon Journal; clicking a slot drives the drop finder

## 0.2.0
- ForeverDungeonJournal link: item sources, click to open the journal, favourites into gear sets, drop finder by slot and armour type, weekly dungeon runs

## 0.1.0
- Ready, Gear, Lockouts, Prep and Settings tabs
- Warband-wide raid readiness, gear plans with plan sharing, lockouts and raid night prep
